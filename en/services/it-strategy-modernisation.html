IT STRATEGY & MODERNISATION

A clear direction for your IT. So your business can keep moving.

A good IT strategy starts with your business. We establish an overview of systems, dependencies and possibilities, clarify important decisions and develop the next sensible steps with you. During implementation, we take on the responsibility we agree – and review together whether the path still fits.

WHEN IT DECISIONS ARE NEEDED

Perhaps you are facing one of these questions.

Your IT has grown. But where should it go next?

New requirements meet existing systems, contracts and responsibilities. There is no shared picture of what can remain – and where change is needed.

The software works. Every change gets harder.

Business-critical applications work, but knowledge is dispersed and dependencies are barely documented. You want to modernise without putting ongoing operations at risk.

Standard software has become a custom solution.

Customisations complicate updates, increase costs or deepen dependence on vendors. A realistic assessment of the actual options is now needed.

The direction is clear. But the initiative has stalled.

Business departments, IT and partners are involved, but priorities and responsibilities do not align. Decisions need to take effect in everyday work again.

WHAT MATTERS

A good IT strategy creates room to act.

It does not need to predict everything. It should help assess options and make important decisions transparently, even when new insights emerge.

Clarity on what matters.

Which systems, risks and dependencies are critical to your business?

Well-founded priorities.

What is worthwhile in view of benefits, costs and risks – and what is not?

Next steps that can be adapted.

Who takes on what, and when is it worth reviewing the path?

OUR SUPPORT

We step in where you need us.

Sometimes an independent perspective is enough. Sometimes a shared IT strategy or support during implementation is needed. Scope and approach depend on your situation.

Assessing the IT situation

We examine your systems, business requirements, risks and dependencies. Together, we clarify which information is missing for a decision and which options are already apparent.

Possible work outcomes: A clear starting point · well-founded options for action.

Aligning strategy and modernisation

We clarify with you what your IT should deliver in future and assess existing solutions, alternatives and possible stages. Architecture, costs, security and your organisation's capabilities inform the decisions.

Possible work outcomes: A shared target vision · clear priorities · next steps that can be reviewed.

Supporting implementation and taking IT responsibility

We bring people together, help put agreed decisions into practice and make progress and obstacles visible. New insights may change the next step. Where needed, we take on clearly agreed IT leadership responsibility for a limited period.

Possible work outcomes: Clear responsibilities · visible progress · timely decisions.

Software development remains with your teams, existing partners or specialists brought in as needed. We take responsibility for the tasks we agree together.

MODERNISATION AS A STRATEGIC DECISION

Not everything needs replacing. But it does need a conscious decision.

Sometimes targeted development is right, sometimes incremental renewal or replacement. What matters is your business, the risks, the costs and your organisation's capabilities.

  • Business value
  • Risks
  • Economic viability
  • Feasibility

  • Develop selectively

    What works reliably does not need to be replaced. We clarify where improvements make sense and what should be preserved.

  • Modernise incrementally

    When parts of an application increasingly slow things down, we plan manageable changes while keeping ongoing operations in view.

  • Replace deliberately

    When effort, risks or dependencies are no longer sustainable, we assess an orderly replacement – including the knowledge that must be preserved.

These options are not mutually exclusive. The right path may change as new insights emerge.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions about working together

Do we already need to know what we want to modernise?

No. Particularly when the direction is still open, assessing the situation together can be useful. We examine the starting point and goals, assess options and clarify which decision comes next.

Do you work with our existing teams and service providers?

Yes. We involve the people who know the systems and processes. Depending on the assignment, we help with strategy and coordination or take on the agreed management of the initiative. We involve additional specialists only when needed.

Can you also take on IT responsibility temporarily?

Yes, provided the task, mandate and decision-making scope are clear. This could, for example, involve managing a modernisation initiative or a time-limited IT leadership role.
