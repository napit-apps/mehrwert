ORGANISATION & SOFTWARE DELIVERY

Making strategy work in everyday life. And helping teams deliver reliably.

Even good teams stall when priorities, dependencies and decisions do not align. We bring leaders and teams together, support concrete improvements in everyday work and review together what changes as a result. We adapt the approach to your organisation and new insights.

WHEN COLLABORATION BECOMES COMPLEX

Do these situations sound familiar?

Every team has a plan. A shared one is missing.

Priorities and dependencies are coordinated too late. What makes sense for one team does not always fit the whole – and shared commitments become uncertain.

Project plans are in place. Actual delivery remains hard to assess.

Budgets and schedules are planned. However, bottlenecks and new insights from development reach project management too late to make timely decisions about priorities, scope or dates.

Agile ways of working are in place. Yet things still stall.

Different quality standards and unclear handovers lead to questions and rework. Often, shared agreements that are actually applied in everyday work are missing.

Improvements are known. Much remains undone in everyday work.

Good ideas from retrospectives or workshops do not progress when time, responsibilities or support are missing. Leadership and teams need to make change possible together.

WHAT MATTERS

Good collaboration shows in work moving forward.

From customer need to finished software, work should flow through the organisation as smoothly as possible. Teams need a shared direction, clear agreements and the ability to identify obstacles early.

Shared direction

Clarify priorities and dependencies across team boundaries.

Less friction

Simplify handovers and reduce queries and waiting times.

Better decisions

Make progress and obstacles visible and adjust together.

OUR SUPPORT

What we can improve together.

Sometimes shared planning across several teams is needed. Sometimes it is daily ways of working or implementing long-known improvements. We focus on what helps your organisation now.

Connecting goals, planning and teams

We coordinate priorities with leaders, product owners and teams, make dependencies visible and create planning that provides direction in everyday work. Whether quarterly planning or a simpler coordination format: we align collaboration with your organisation.

Possible work outcomes: Agreed goals and priorities · visible dependencies · suitable planning and feedback formats.

Improving ways of working and quality

Together, we examine queries, waiting times and rework – for example due to unclear requirements or handovers. With those involved, we develop practical agreements and support their use in everyday work.

Possible work outcomes: Better-prepared work · shared quality criteria · clearer handovers.

Supporting improvements and reviewing their effect

We support leadership and teams in applying changes in everyday work. Together, we review whether, for example, lead times, rework or delivery reliability improve. We use suitable metrics and feedback and adapt measures when they do not have the intended effect.

Possible work outcomes: Concrete improvements with named owners · regular feedback · suitable measurement signals for decisions.

SOFTWARE DELIVERY IN TRANSITION

When AI changes development, collaboration needs to evolve with it.

AI can change software analysis, development, testing and documentation. This raises new questions: who checks results? How is quality maintained? What should coordination and feedback look like when work changes?

We consider tools and collaboration together. Whether responsibilities, quality checks or ways of working should be adapted depends on actual capabilities and requirements. Value, security and traceable results remain decisive.

COLLABORATION

Change needs people who take responsibility.

We take responsibility for the agreed tasks: we develop suitable measures with those involved, support their introduction, address obstacles openly and adjust when needed.

For improvements to endure in everyday work, leadership must also set priorities, make decisions and allow time for change. We work with your teams and existing partners – selectively or over a longer period, depending on the task.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions about working together

Do our teams already need to work in an agile way?

No. What matters is not which process model you use today, but which problems you want to solve. We build on existing structures and develop with you the ways of working that actually help.

Do we need to fundamentally change our existing planning and ways of working?

Not necessarily. We start with what already works and consider together where changes make sense. Project and portfolio planning, teams' daily work and management oversight do not have to follow the same method. What matters is that they fit together and enable necessary decisions in time.

How do we recognise whether software delivery is improving?

We first look at the effect you want to achieve – such as better predictability, less waiting or higher quality. We select suitable observations and metrics accordingly. They should support teams in improving and enable informed decisions, not pit individuals or teams against each other.

Who ensures that new ways of working are used over the long term?

We design and support the introduction, review application and make obstacles visible. Management and the respective teams are needed for priorities, necessary capacity and binding decisions. We help build precisely this collaboration from the outset.
