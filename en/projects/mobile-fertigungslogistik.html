PROFESSIONAL EXPERIENCE · MARCEL RUSS

Mobile production logistics in agricultural machinery manufacturing

Kverneland

Understand production workflows, design a mobile solution for them and introduce it under actual manufacturing conditions.

Android application · Process understanding · Technical coordination · On-site introduction

The right part needs to be in the right place at the right time.

In agricultural machinery manufacturing, pre-assembly and main production must be closely coordinated. Required components need to be available where they will be needed next in production. Information about requirements and supply must therefore reach the relevant areas reliably.

The task was to support this coordination with a mobile application. The solution had to fit existing workflows and remain practical where production network connectivity was not consistently stable.

My task: connect production realities with mobile technology.

In an earlier professional role, I contributed to the technical design and coordination of an Android solution for this production process. Together with those involved, we examined workflows, specified requirements and aligned user interaction and technical architecture with production use.

Alongside development coordination, this included working with international development partners. It mattered to me to support the introduction directly in the production setting, rather than planning the application solely from a distance.

A good app begins with understanding work on site.

The specific manufacturing conditions were central to this initiative. The technical solution had to support the process rather than create additional work for people.

  1. 01

    Understand workflows and information needs

    What information do pre-assembly and main production need, and when? These questions formed the basis for aligning functions and interaction with actual production steps.

  2. 02

    Make mobile architecture practical for daily use

    The Android application's user interaction and technical implementation were adapted to the working environment. Because Wi-Fi was not reliably available everywhere, the application had to cope with interruptions. Local caching was an important technical element.

  3. 03

    Coordinate development and support introduction

    Implementation was technically supported and coordinated with the development partners involved. On-site introduction helped test the application at the actual workplace and align it with manufacturing workflows.

Mobile information connects two production areas.

The application supports information flow between work steps. What matters is keeping required data available under changing conditions.

What emerged from the initiative.

Joint work on processes and technology produced an Android application that was introduced and used in production.

An Android application in production use

The solution supported coordination between pre-assembly and main production on a production line. The mobile application was not only designed, but introduced into practical work.

Technology for real operating conditions

The architecture accounted for incomplete network coverage in production. An operational constraint thus became part of the technical solution early on.

What makes this experience valuable in my work today.

A technical solution is good only when it works where it is needed. This requires understanding the workflows it supports, the local constraints and the long-term implications for operation and development.

I bring this experience to strategy and modernisation work at mehr.wert: considering architecture decisions in relation to business processes and planning realistic implementation from the outset.

Does your software need to work under demanding conditions?

Whether in production, logistics or another business-critical workflow, we are happy to discuss how technical requirements fit the realities of your organisation.
