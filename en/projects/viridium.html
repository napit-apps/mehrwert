CLIENT ENGAGEMENT · MEHR.WERT

Shared working foundations for twelve squads

Viridium

Align different teams around shared quality standards and more reliable collaboration, while supporting the changes in everyday work.

Organisational development · Software delivery · Ongoing engagement

Many teams. Different ways of working. A shared delivery task.

An IT organisation with twelve squads brings together different domain tasks, working practices and project requirements. What works well in one team is not automatically understandable or compatible across team and release boundaries.

The challenge was not to introduce yet another agile framework. Shared foundations for requirements, quality and planning were needed, together with a connection between project expectations, release coordination and what the squads could actually deliver. Above all, these foundations needed to be usable in daily work.

Our task: establish shared foundations and support their use.

mehr.wert supports the organisation in developing its collaboration and software delivery. We connect observations from teams with leadership requirements, make areas for action visible and develop concrete, practical proposals.

We take responsibility for developing, introducing and supporting these proposals in practice. This includes addressing obstacles, incorporating everyday feedback and refining improvements. Decisions on priorities, capacity and binding expectations remain with management, and we actively request them when they are needed for implementation.

From shared understanding to use within a squad.

We deliberately focused on places where a shared framework could make daily work easier. The measures complement one another: exchange, concrete quality foundations and feedback on their value.

  1. 01

    Connect teams and those responsible

    A community of practice provides a place where experiences from the squads come together and shared working practices are developed. Participants review proposals, contribute their perspectives and take results back to their teams.

  2. 02

    Make work and quality easier to understand

    We developed and published shared foundations for requirement and change tickets, backlog preparation, refinement, relative estimation and the Definition of Done. This makes expectations of prepared work and finished results clearer across squad boundaries.

  3. 03

    Support application and make progress visible

    We support adoption through direct work with squads, targeted training and a structured examination of existing practices. An agile assessment helps identify differences and obstacles and prioritise improvements. Feedback from practice informs the next steps.

Shared working practices emerge through interaction.

Cross-team agreements create value only when teams understand and apply them, and experience from daily work feeds back into them.

What has already been established.

The shared foundations have been developed and initial applications are visible in daily work. Embedding them across the organisation is a further step that we actively support.

Organised exchange across squad boundaries

The community of practice brings different roles and teams together. It provides a setting for combining experience and developing shared working practices beyond individual squads.

Usable working and quality foundations

Templates and guidelines have been documented, presented and taken up by the first squads. One example showed how quickly shared direction can become practical application: after story-point training, a squad independently used the method in refinement the following day.

A basis for targeted development

A structured assessment and team feedback help select the next improvements more consciously. The actual effect on software delivery is reviewed as the engagement continues and cannot be inferred solely from introducing shared standards.

What makes this experience relevant to other organisations.

Organisational development does not end with a guideline or a good workshop. It becomes effective where leaders create the necessary conditions and teams can incorporate changes into their daily work.

At mehr.wert, we therefore combine shared direction with practical implementation support. Good methods help; what matters is whether they actually make a difference in the organisation concerned.

Are your teams committed, but not yet working well enough together?

When standards vary, handovers take time or improvements fail to reach everyday work, it is worth examining the causes together. We help develop suitable foundations and support their use.
