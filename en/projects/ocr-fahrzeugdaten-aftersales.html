PROFESSIONAL EXPERIENCE · MARCEL RUSS

Capture vehicle data with a smartphone

Capture registration plates and vehicle identification numbers during vehicle reception using mobile text recognition, instead of relying entirely on manual transcription.

Automotive · iOS · Camera and optical text recognition

An everyday task where many small steps matter.

Information must be recorded reliably during vehicle reception. This includes the registration plate and vehicle identification number. Transcribing these manually requires attention and may lead to additional questions or corrections.

The task was to support this capture step with a smartphone. The camera was to recognise characters and make them available as digital information. What mattered was fitting this technical capability into the work on site.

My contribution: implement mobile text recognition.

In an earlier professional role, I contributed to the technical design and development of an iOS application for this use case. This included investigating mobile image recognition for registration plates and vehicle identification numbers and turning it into a usable application.

The interesting question was not simply whether a camera could recognise characters. It was about supporting a specific capture step with an appropriate mobile solution. The technical design therefore followed the intended workflow.

A camera image becomes usable information.

The solution combined image capture, optical character recognition and presentation of the captured vehicle data in an iOS application.

  1. 01

    Understand the capture use case

    Registration plates and VINs differ in length and presentation. The mobile application needed clarity on which information to capture and how the step fitted into vehicle reception.

  2. 02

    Integrate mobile image recognition

    The iOS application used the camera and optical text recognition to capture characters from vehicle data. I contributed to the technical design and implementation of this functionality.

  3. 03

    Make recognised data available in the application

    A recognised character is helpful only when it becomes understandable information. Integrating recognition into the mobile capture workflow was therefore part of the task.

The capture workflow in three steps.

The technical principle is straightforward: the camera supplies an image, the software recognises characters, and the application provides the captured information.

What emerged from the project.

The technical work produced a functioning iOS application for recognising and capturing vehicle data. The project demonstrated how smartphone image processing can support a practical task in the automotive sector.

Its professional value lies in combining mobile technology, a concrete understanding of the process and a working implementation, from image capture to available information.

What I take from this into today's software initiatives.

Not every improvement needs a large platform or a comprehensive transformation programme. Sometimes a well-designed technical function makes an important task simpler.

In my work at mehr.wert today, the wider context matters: what do people on site need, which technology suits that need, and how can its quality be assessed under real conditions?
