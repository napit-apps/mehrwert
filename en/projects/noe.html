Client engagement · mehr.wert

Independent IT assessment for informed decisions

NOE

When IT projects stall, an independent outside perspective can sometimes help. In a focused assessment, we examined a challenging IT and ERP project situation, identified areas for action and developed specific recommendations for its ongoing management.

IT assessment · Project management · Organisational development

Initial situation

When IT faces difficult decisions.

Complex IT and ERP initiatives involve more than technical requirements. Responsibilities, collaboration and project management also need to work effectively.

NOE needed an independent assessment of its existing situation. The focus was on the IT and project organisation, existing risks and how to approach further development sensibly.

Our contribution

Two days, an independent perspective and specific recommendations.

We examined the situation during a two-day on-site analysis and structured the key areas for action.

We focused on three questions:

Management and transparency

How is the initiative organised, and where are there risks or uncertainties for its ongoing management?

Responsibilities and collaboration

How are tasks, responsibilities and decisions defined within the IT and project organisation?

Sensible next steps

What options emerge from the assessment, and what should be considered as development continues?

Based on this analysis, we developed specific recommendations and presented them to the client.

Results

A basis for the next decisions.

The assessment provided a structured evaluation of the initial situation, identified areas for action and specific recommendations for the future IT and project organisation.

This gave the client additional information and an independent perspective for upcoming decisions.

The engagement ended with the presentation of the findings. We do not know which recommendations were subsequently implemented or what impact they had.

What we take away

Not every challenge needs a large consulting project.

Sometimes a focused, independent assessment is enough to put risks and possible courses of action into perspective.

What matters is looking closely, asking the right questions and developing recommendations that fit the company’s actual situation.

Are you facing difficult IT decisions?

An extensive project is not always needed straight away. Let’s talk about the questions on your mind and whether an independent perspective on your situation could help.
