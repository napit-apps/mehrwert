CLIENT ENGAGEMENT · MEHR.WERT

Modernising a business-critical software platform

Dücker

Preparing established software for the future – by bringing business departments and development together to create a shared basis for decisions and implementation.

Ongoing modernisation initiative · Business and technical collaboration · IT strategy

The application is indispensable. Knowledge about it is dispersed.

When software supports a business for many years, it often contains much more than program code. Established workflows, business decisions and special rules are part of day-to-day operations. Not all of this is fully documented outside the application.

In this initiative, different departments brought their own perspectives on the existing platform. A shared understanding of the domain and a common modernisation path first had to be developed. The task was to prepare necessary changes without losing the valuable knowledge held in the current system.

Our task: bringing people together and preparing concrete steps towards modernisation.

We support the company in preparing the business and strategic foundations for its software modernisation. We connect the perspectives of business departments and development, structure open questions and help turn different expectations into a jointly supported direction.

An important part of our responsibility is to prepare the outcomes so they can be used in further development, beyond the initial discussions. Technical implementation remains with the development teams involved. mehr.wert supports the agreed modernisation work and connects business clarification with preparation for implementation.

First understand what needs to be preserved. Then determine the next step.

The focus was not on a premature decision for a new technology. We worked on the foundations that make informed modernisation possible in the first place.

  1. 01

    Creating a shared understanding of the domain

    People from departments that had previously worked separately came together to examine business processes, terminology and relationships. This enabled them to clarify different perspectives and build a shared understanding of business requirements.

  2. 02

    Making existing business rules accessible

    Important business rules were embedded in the existing software. We identified and structured these rules and handed them over to development. Knowledge from the existing system thus became a concrete basis for the next technical steps.

  3. 03

    Agreeing a viable modernisation path

    A modernisation strategy was developed together on this basis. It connects business requirements with implementation options and provides guidance for taking the initiative forward step by step.

Business knowledge becomes the basis for implementation.

Connecting business departments and development is not an additional coordination step. It is a prerequisite for preserving and changing the right things during modernisation.

What has already been established.

Modernisation is an ongoing initiative. Key foundations for the next steps have already been created together.

A shared understanding of the business domain

Previously separate departments brought their perspectives together and developed a shared understanding of processes and requirements. This provides a better basis for business discussions and further decisions.

An agreed direction for modernisation

Those involved defined a common path for the platform's further development. It provides direction without pre-empting specific implementation steps.

Business rules prepared for development

Business rules from the existing application were identified and handed over to development. This material can help preserve established behaviour in a traceable way as the system evolves incrementally.

What makes this experience relevant to other modernisation initiatives.

The hardest part of modernisation is not always the new technology. Often, the first step is to understand why an existing application works the way it does – and which business dependencies are involved.

That is why we consider IT strategy, the business domain and implementation together. We help develop a responsible way forward and establish the foundations for changes to go beyond a target architecture on paper.

Is a business-critical application in your organisation facing change?

Whether modernising, incrementally developing or replacing it: a clear understanding of the starting point comes first. Let us discuss the decisions facing your initiative.
