CLIENT ENGAGEMENT · MEHR.WERT

Roadmap for AI-assisted software development

HUGO BOSS

Assess the possibilities of AI in software development together, clarify priorities and establish a viable direction for the next steps.

Strategic team workshop · Software development lifecycle · 2026

Many good ideas need a shared direction.

An experienced, technology-minded team wanted to explore new possibilities for the software development lifecycle. Expertise and enthusiasm for innovation were already present. The challenge was to align individual perspectives around a common goal and clarify the specific value the planned changes should create for their audiences.

AI opened up new options. But before choosing tools or building solutions, fundamental questions need answers: who are we developing for? What problem do we want to solve? What takes priority, and how will we later know whether the chosen direction is worthwhile?

Our task: provide direction and constructively challenge ideas.

mehr.wert designed, prepared and facilitated the strategic workshop. We were not responsible for technical implementation, but for creating a working setting in which an experienced team could bring its ideas together and prepare concrete decisions.

This involved targeted questions, critical discussion and the ability to address topics as they emerged without losing sight of the shared outcome. Our aim was for the exchange to produce more than a collection of interesting ideas.

From team purpose to concrete next steps.

Five fundamental management questions provided guidance: what is our mission? Who do we work for? What value do we create? How do we measure progress? How do we proceed? These questions helped align technical possibilities with the team's actual task.

  1. 01

    Clarify the task and audience

    Participants brought together their perspectives on the initiative and discussed the contribution their team should make. This exposed differing expectations and helped align them around a shared understanding.

  2. 02

    Assess value and priorities together

    Technology ideas, including AI-assisted approaches in development, were connected with expected customer value and organisational prerequisites. Not every exciting possibility needed to become part of the immediate next step.

  3. 03

    Develop a roadmap

    The discussions produced an understandable direction with prioritised topics and next steps. The roadmap provides guidance for further work while leaving room to test assumptions in practice.

New technologies need good decisions first.

The path from a technological possibility to a meaningful initiative begins with a shared understanding of its purpose.

What the workshop achieved.

The workshop's value lay in jointly developed direction and a basis for further work, rather than in a new method.

A shared view of the initiative

The team was able to align goals and expectations. Feedback explicitly confirmed that the workshop helped develop a shared view of the project.

A structured roadmap

Participants developed a roadmap for the next steps. Its value was also explicitly confirmed in client feedback. This provided a concrete work product to guide further development.

What makes this experience relevant to new technology initiatives.

When AI or other technological developments open up possibilities, many ideas quickly emerge. The harder question is often which of them fit a company's goals, people and capabilities.

Our work therefore combines technical curiosity with strategic clarity. We help assess opportunities, establish a shared direction and prepare next steps so that a team can continue independently.
