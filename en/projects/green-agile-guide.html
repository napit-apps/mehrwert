Green Agile Guide – Sustainability in agile ways of working

Agile methods help teams learn quickly and improve their software. Yet the long-term environmental, economic and social impacts often go unconsidered.

The Green Agile Guide shows how sustainability can become part of existing agile ways of working without introducing additional roles, meetings or frameworks.

Explore the Green Agile Guide

Consider sustainability where decisions are made.

Sustainability does not have to be a separate task alongside product development. The guide builds on familiar agile goals, roles and practices by bringing in questions about long-term value, resource consumption and responsibility.

It focuses on three areas:

Awareness and responsibility.

Two values form the foundation of the Green Agile Guide.

Awareness

Recognise how our decisions affect people, the environment and economic viability, and make trade-offs visible.

Responsibility

Turn these insights into decisions and actions, then check whether they have the intended effect.

Sustainable software takes shape where awareness meets responsibility.

Explore the Green Agile Guide.

The guide is an independent initiative started by mehr.wert. It continues to evolve as a contribution to sustainable software development.
