PROFESSIONAL EXPERIENCE · MARCEL RUSS

Motion analysis with a smart sports shirt and app

HUGO BOSS

An innovation initiative combining sensors, algorithms and mobile software, with different technical specialists working on a shared product idea.

HUGO BOSS · Innovation and development · Mobile software · Sensors

Capture movement and make it understandable through an app.

The idea was to capture movements during training using a smart sports shirt and display them in a mobile application. Different technical elements had to work together: sensors provide information, algorithms process it and the app makes it understandable to people.

This combination made the initiative challenging. It was not just about a single application, but about different disciplines collaborating on an early product idea.

Connect mobile development with specialists and partners.

During my employment at HUGO BOSS, I was involved in this innovation initiative within the technical development organisation. My work concerned the interaction between mobile software development, internal teams, nearshore developers and specialist partners.

Different perspectives had to come together: app requirements, the capabilities of sensors and algorithms, and practical coordination among those involved. Development was a joint effort by various specialists, not a product I developed alone.

Three parts of a shared product idea.

Three connected perspectives help explain the technical challenge.

  1. 01

    Capture movement information

    The sensors in the garment were intended to provide movement information during training. Which data can be used meaningfully depends on the technical capabilities and the intended use.

  2. 02

    Process information algorithmically

    Captured information does not yet provide understandable feedback. The data must be processed appropriately and the results made usable for the intended presentation.

  3. 03

    Display results in a mobile app

    The application was intended to visualise movements. A shared understanding of the information to be displayed was therefore important for coordination between app development and the other technical disciplines.

From movement to mobile presentation.

The concept connected several technical elements into an understandable processing chain.

What this work demonstrated.

At HUGO BOSS, an innovation initiative combined smart clothing, sensors, algorithmic analysis and mobile visualisation. I was involved in technical coordination and collaboration between the participating teams and partners.

The experience primarily concerns the interaction of different technical disciplines and organisational arrangements. Whether and in what form a market-ready product later emerged is not part of this account.

Why I bring these experiences to today's consulting work.

New technologies often look particularly promising when viewed as individual components. In a real product, however, these components must work together, and the people involved must develop a shared understanding of goals and limitations.

In my work at mehr.wert today, this experience helps me consider new technical possibilities in relation to product goals, integration effort and the capabilities of the teams involved, rather than focusing on the technology alone.

Do you want to assess how new technology fits into an initiative?

We consider technical possibilities, organisational prerequisites and actual value together, with a clear view of the next sensible steps.
