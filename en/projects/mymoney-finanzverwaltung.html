OWN PRODUCT · MARCEL RUSS

myMoney – keeping personal finances in view

A self-developed iOS app for recording, analysing and clearly presenting income and expenses, from the product idea to ongoing development under my own responsibility.

iOS software product · Product concept · Swift · Data and user experience

Turn numbers into an understandable overview.

People who want to record personal income and expenses primarily need a clear overview. Information must be easy to enter, meaningfully displayed and easy to find again. The technical solution should support this everyday task without unnecessary complexity.

With myMoney, I developed my own mobile application for this task. It combines personal financial data capture with analyses that help users understand the available information.

In a product of your own, all decisions are connected.

I conceived, designed and developed myMoney for iOS myself. This included user-experience requirements, technical architecture, data processing and publication in the App Store.

I was also responsible for subsequent maintenance. I could not simply hand over a finished development version: decisions about functionality, usability and technical development had to fit together throughout the product lifecycle.

Easy to use. Technically well considered.

In a finance app, understandable interfaces and reliable handling of user data come together directly. Three aspects were particularly formative for me.

  1. 01

    Design capture and interaction

    The interface was intended to help users record income and expenses without unnecessary steps. I designed workflows, interfaces and data presentation as one integrated product task.

  2. 02

    Store, analyse and provide data

    iOS development included local data storage, analysis and visualisation functions, and export options. Data processing and understandable presentation therefore had to work reliably together.

  3. 03

    Publish and maintain

    Publication in the App Store turned the development into a usable software product. Ongoing maintenance and product decisions were as much a part of my work as the original technical implementation.

From input to understandable analysis.

The core of the application is turning information entered by users into a useful overview.

An iOS product of my own, from idea to publication.

The product idea became a published iOS app for managing personal finances. I was responsible for the concept, interface design and technical development. Ongoing maintenance brought further tasks beyond a one-off implementation.

myMoney is therefore a second, distinct product experience alongside myWork: a different user need, but the same responsibility for ensuring usability, functions and technical foundations continue to fit together over time.

Why my own products also shape me as a consultant.

Developing and maintaining a product yourself means seeing requirements beyond implementation. Ease of use, data quality, long-term maintenance and the effort involved in new features all matter in each decision.

I bring this perspective to mehr.wert today: a technical solution should not just look convincing on paper, but work in its intended everyday setting and remain manageable over the long term.

We consider technology, usage and further development together so that solutions fit your company and its requirements.
