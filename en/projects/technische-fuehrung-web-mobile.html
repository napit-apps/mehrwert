PROFESSIONAL EXPERIENCE · MARCEL RUSS

Leading web and mobile development

HUGO BOSS

Taking responsibility for digital products and development teams – bringing together technical quality, people and organisational collaboration.

HUGO BOSS · Team Lead Web & Mobile · 2018–2022

Digital products need more than good individual decisions.

Work on web and mobile products connects many perspectives: technical development, business requirements, user experience, product decisions and ongoing operations. In an international organisation, different teams, locations and partners are also involved.

For technical leadership, this means more than coordinating individual development tasks. Products and teams need a shared direction and decisions that fit the company's goals and development capabilities.

My role: responsibility for teams, products and technical decisions.

At HUGO BOSS, I led web and mobile development for several years. My role included technical and line management of employees, responsibility for digital products and technical coordination with those involved.

I experienced directly how closely technology and leadership are connected. Product decisions influence teams' work; technical dependencies affect priorities and possibilities. Conversely, good collaboration helps determine whether an idea becomes a viable solution.

Keeping technology, products and people in view together.

This responsibility could not be divided into separate technical and organisational tasks. The three perspectives belonged together in everyday work.

  1. 01

    Providing technical direction

    Preparing and coordinating technical decisions, supporting the development of web and mobile applications and connecting architecture and quality questions with product requirements. Specific topics in this setting ranged from reusable UI components to considered handling of third-party dependencies in mobile applications.

  2. 02

    Taking responsibility for digital products

    Connecting product goals, requirements and technical implementation. This involves not only new features, but also viable decisions for maintenance, further development and ongoing operations.

  3. 03

    Leading teams and enabling collaboration

    Providing technical and line management, shaping coordination between teams and locations and involving internal and external development partners in the shared work.

Three perspectives. One shared responsibility.

Leadership grounded in software development.

Above all, I take from this period the experience of taking responsibility not only for a technical solution, but also for the people and the organisation developing it.

Several years of team leadership

I led web and mobile teams over several years, combining technical tasks, people management and collaboration.

Technical and product responsibility

Decisions about technology and digital products were part of the same leadership role. This means I know both the technical details and the wider requirements for reliable product development.

Collaboration across locations

Working with distributed teams and development partners was part of everyday leadership. Shared direction, clear coordination and trust are just as important as technical expertise.

Why this period shapes my work today.

In consulting engagements, it is easy to talk about processes, architecture or leadership without bearing their everyday tensions yourself. From my time as a developer and team leader, I know these tensions through personal responsibility.

At mehr.wert, this experience helps me consider technical decisions and organisational changes together. I can connect the perspectives of management, product owners and development teams and support changes so they can work under real working conditions.

Develop technical leadership and software delivery together?

When teams, products and technology decisions are closely connected, it helps to look at the whole system. Let us discuss where leadership and collaboration in your IT organisation could become more effective.
