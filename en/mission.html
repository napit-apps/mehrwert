ABOUT MEHR.WERT

Sustainably better software. Out of conviction.

For us, software is never just technology. It influences how people work, how companies make decisions and how easily things can change. We want to help good ideas become something that holds up in everyday life. With our own experience, an open perspective and a willingness to take responsibility.

WHY MEHR.WERT EXISTS

Why I founded mehr.wert.

I began my career as a software developer and consultant. Even before my time at HUGO BOSS, I worked in very different industries – including automotive and industrial settings. I repeatedly had to familiarise myself with new domains, workflows and people. This shaped my perspective: good software does not come from good technology alone. You need to understand what it is needed for and who will work with it later.

With my app myWork, I also developed a product of my own, continued developing it over many years and operated it. This gives you direct experience of how long technical decisions have an impact, how requirements change and how important feedback is from people who use an application every day.

Later, at HUGO BOSS, I led web and mobile teams and took responsibility for digital products. At Exxeta and in EnBW Trading IT, modernisation, strategy and collaboration in larger organisations were added. One thing kept recurring: there is rarely a shortage of good ideas or capable people. Things become harder when decisions remain unresolved, responsibilities are unclear or changes fail to take effect in everyday work.

I founded mehr.wert in 2024 because I wanted to bring these experiences together. I want to support companies independently, express my assessment openly and take responsibility for the work we agree together. For me, this is not about changing as much as possible. It is about finding out what can actually improve – and what already works well and should remain.

I believe in experience, but not in ready-made answers for every situation. That is why I listen, question my own assumptions too and remain open to new insights. Together with the people who know the company and its challenges best, we find the right path.

Marcel Russ · Founder and Managing Director

Incidentally, the name mehr.wert goes back to a client who once said I was worth more than all the expensive consultants from the big firms put together – it made me smile, but the aspiration has stayed with me.

HOW WE ARE SET UP

A stable core. A network that fits the task.

mehr.wert is deliberately personally led. I am your direct contact, develop the direction with you and shape our collaboration. I take responsibility for the tasks we agree – from the first conversation to the next steps in practice.

Not every initiative needs the same people or a large project team. When additional experience or specialist technical skills are needed, I work with specialists from my network. This creates a group assembled according to need: with a clear core, complementary expertise and as little organisational overhead as makes sense.

Your own teams and existing partners remain involved. We complement what is missing instead of replacing structures that work. And even when other specialists contribute, you always know whom to contact.

HOW WE WORK

We bring experience. And remain open to what we learn.

At the beginning, I want to understand what is on your mind, what already works well and where you would like change. We look at technical questions, but equally at the people who will later work with the results. Your industry, culture and available possibilities matter more than any standard model.

Sometimes what is needed above all is a clear decision. Sometimes a small, manageable experiment helps before considerable money and time are committed. And sometimes the best recommendation is deliberately not to change something. We use the ways of working and methods that help in the particular situation – and leave the rest out.

When we change something, we are interested in its consequences in everyday work. We gather feedback, examine suitable metrics and speak openly about what works and what does not. The next steps follow from this. Together, with clear responsibilities and a willingness to correct course.

WHAT MATTERS TO US

Sustainably better. For businesses, people and the environment.

For us, sustainably better software means more than lower costs or a longer lifespan. We want to help shape solutions that make economic sense, support people and use our planet's resources responsibly. For us, all three perspectives belong in a good decision.

Economic

Software should bring real value and remain viable over the long term. We therefore consider not only development costs, but also operation, maintainability, dependencies and the possibilities for making changes later.

Social

Software is developed by people and for people. We consider how it affects everyday work, whether it is accessible and understandable and how teams collaborate. Good solutions should support people, not create additional barriers.

Environmental

Software also consumes energy and resources. Computing power, data transfer, infrastructure and the lifespan of systems affect our environment. We want to consider these effects consciously and improve them where it is sensible and possible.

For us, the planet is a stakeholder too – even if it never sits in any of our meetings.

Awareness and responsibility from Green Agile help us bring these perspectives together. We examine opportunities and trade-offs, explain our recommendations and take responsibility for the agreed tasks. Not everything can be optimised at once. But we should know the consequences of our decisions.

This approach also shapes our own collaboration. We use proven agile approaches and new technologies where they help and review together whether they deliver the intended value.
